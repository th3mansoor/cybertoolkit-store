IMPORTANT CONTACT FORM LIMITATION
The contact form is intentionally a front-end prototype. It does not send or store messages yet. A delivery endpoint cannot be configured responsibly until a real contact email is available and a free provider has been chosen. The form tells visitors that their message was not sent.

IMPORTANT PRIVACY POLICY LIMITATION
The privacy policy is a starter template and must be reviewed and completed before enabling advertising, analytics, affiliate tracking, or a live contact form. It is not legal advice. Do not claim that a feature or data practice exists unless it is actually enabled and verified.

This batch does not add paid services, analytics, ad scripts, tracking, or API keys.
